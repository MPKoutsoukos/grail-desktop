# The grail name and look

The code in this repository (the desktop app; grail-desktop) is free software under the [GNU GPL v3](LICENSE): you may use, study, change and
share it under that licence. The licence covers the code. It doesn't cover the **grail name and brand**, which
belong to MPKoutsoukos:

- the name **grail** for a notes app, in any form ("grail", "grail notes", "grail notebook");
- the grail **wordmark** (the word "grail" in gold, as on the app icon) and the app icons;
- the **grailnotebook.com** domain and the app IDs `com.grailnotebook.*`.

## What's fine

- Talking about grail, writing about it, linking to it, reviewing it.
- Using the code under the GPL, and saying truthfully that your project is based on grail ("based on grail",
  "a fork of grail").
- Building grail from this source for yourself, unchanged, under its own name.

## What needs permission

- Publishing a changed version, or any app, under the name grail or a confusingly similar name, or with the
  grail wordmark or icons. A fork is welcome: give it its own name, its own icon and its own app ID.
- Implying that your version is the official grail, or that it's endorsed by its maker.

The official apps are the ones on this repository's Releases page, on
[grail](https://github.com/MPKoutsoukos/grail)'s (the Android app), and (once published) on Google Play under
the grailnotebook.com developer.
